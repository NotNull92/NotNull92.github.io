Monstel Privacy Policy

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Operator and Scope

  • 지영준 (the “Operator”) provides this policy on the processing of personal information in the mobile game Monstel. The chief privacy officer and contact person is 지영준, who can be reached at fatiger92@gmail.com.
  • To play the game, you are not asked to enter your name, email address or phone number, and no separate account sign-up is required. However, the information below may be processed for purchase verification, optional cloud save, advertising, and inquiries you send to us. Having no sign-up does not mean that no personal information is processed at all.
  • Game data is stored by default in the app's private storage on your device. Information kept in Google storage when cloud save is turned on, on the purchase verification server, and in the inquiry mailbox is described separately.

2. Purposes, Items and Collection Methods

  • On-device game progress: Your progress, Gems and payment receipt records are stored on your device to manage game progress and purchase history. This description alone does not mean that there is no processing outside the device.
  • Google Play billing and purchase verification: When you buy a paid item, Google processes the payment information. The game receives a purchase token and a product ID, and sends the purchase token to the Operator's verification server to confirm the purchase and grant the paid entitlement. The server stores the purchase token and the verification result.
  • Google Play Games cloud save (optional): If you turn on cloud save, game data is kept in the storage of your Google Play Games account, and Google processes your account identifier. It is used only for cloud save, and no connection is made unless you turn it on.
  • AdMob rewarded ads: To display rewarded ads, the advertising ID (GAID) may be passed to Google. Google's SDK disclosures also describe the processing of IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device and account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This is the provider's general description.
  • Customer support, refunds and rights requests: If you contact us by email, your sender email address, the content of your inquiry, and any order information or attachments you provide are used to confirm and answer your request and to handle disputes. Please do not send information that the inquiry does not need, such as your resident registration number or full card number. If further verification of your identity or the transaction is needed, we will request only the minimum information.
  • App usage statistics (sessions): To count the number of users and usage time, when you start and close the game, the install ID, session ID, session start and end times and duration, whether it is the first launch, the app version and the platform (Android) are sent to a collection server run by the Operator (located in the Republic of Korea). The install ID is a random value created when the app is first launched and kept in the app's private storage; it is not linked to device identifiers, the advertising ID or any account. Your name, email address, location and game progress are not sent. If there is no network, the data is held on the device and sent once a connection is available.
  • Notifications (optional): To let you know when the gold earned while you are away has reached its cap and when an unclaimed parcel is about to disappear, notification times and messages are scheduled only on your device. No information is sent to the Operator's server or anywhere else for notifications. The Android notification permission is requested only once, after you finish the tutorial, and declining it does not affect your use of the game.
  • Access logs: When app usage statistics are sent, the access logs at the front end of the collection server record the connecting IP address, the request path and the time. These are used only to protect the server and diagnose failures, are not linked to event records, and are deleted after at most 90 days. IP addresses blocked for abnormal access are kept for about 5 weeks.
  • We disclose the scope and purposes of personal information processing by the applicable SDKs, ad providers, server logs and backups.

3. Legal Bases and Choices

  • Purchase verification, granting entitlements and handling user requests are processed, to the extent necessary to conclude and perform the relevant contract, under lawful bases such as Article 15 of the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법). Statutory transaction records are retained only to the extent necessary under retention obligations such as those of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래법).
  • Optional cloud save, advertising and other data processing follow the legal basis appropriate to each feature and item. Turning on an optional feature, or the publication of this policy alone, is not treated as obtaining every consent that is required.
  • Where consent is required, we inform you of the purposes of collection and use, the items, the retention period, your right to refuse and any disadvantage of refusing, and we process the information only after obtaining your consent. We distinguish required items from optional items, and we do not restrict your use of unrelated basic services solely because you did not consent to optional items.
  • If you do not turn on cloud save, the related sync feature is not used. How to refuse or withdraw other optional items, and the effect on features, are explained in this policy and on the relevant screens in line with the actual features.

4. External Services, Provision to Third Parties and Outsourcing

  • The roles of Google Play billing, Google Play Games and AdMob may differ from one another. We distinguish information that Google processes directly from users from information that the game sends to Google or to the Operator's server. Google's processing is also subject to the terms of the relevant services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Where a provision to a third party applies, we disclose the recipient, purpose, items, retention period and legal basis, and where consent is required, we separately give the notice and obtain the consent needed for the provision.
  • Outsourcing (hosting): The Operator runs the app usage statistics collection server directly and rents the server equipment in the Seoul, Republic of Korea region of Vultr (The Constant Company, LLC, a U.S. corporation). The outsourced work is the provision of server equipment and network, and the data is stored in Korea.
  • Where outsourcing applies, we disclose the outsourcees, any sub-outsourcees and the work involved as required by law, and we put in place the necessary outsourcing contracts and management and supervision measures.

5. Overseas Transfer of Personal Information

  • Google LLC (United States): Google Play billing and purchase verification (purchase token, product ID), Google Play Games cloud save (game data, account identifier), AdMob rewarded ads (advertising ID GAID, IP address, device and ad interaction information). The information is transferred over the network when you use the services and, once the purpose is fulfilled, is kept and deleted according to each service's retention criteria. Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) also applies.
  • Verification server: The purchase verification server has not yet been deployed; once its location is decided, the country and recipient will be added to this item. There is currently no actual overseas transfer.
  • App usage statistics: The collection server is in the Republic of Korea (Seoul), so this is not an overseas transfer. If the server location changes, the country and recipient will be added to this item.
  • Refusal: You can avoid the relevant transfers by not turning on cloud save or by turning off ad personalization. However, purchase verification is essential to providing paid items, so if you refuse it, your use of paid items will be restricted.
  • Transfers are made only where there is a lawful basis and safeguards are in place, such as under Article 28-8 of the Personal Information Protection Act. Where separate consent is required, we notify you and obtain your consent in advance.

6. Retention and Use Period

  • On-device game data: You can delete it by resetting in the game's Settings or by deleting the app. Cloud data, device backups and server records are separate; see Section 7 for whether data is restored after deletion or reinstallation.
  • Purchase verification records: The retention period is 5 years from the payment date and covers the purchase tokens and verification results that fall within the statutory transaction records needed to confirm the contract, payment and supply. This does not mean that all logs are kept for 5 years across the board.
  • App usage statistics: Raw events (including the install ID) are automatically deleted 90 days after receipt. Server backups are kept for 14 days, and backup copies the Operator keeps separately are also not kept for more than 90 days. Daily aggregates (number of users, number of sessions, figures by app version) are kept without identifiers.
  • Statutory transaction records: Under the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce and Article 6 of its Enforcement Decree, records on contracts and withdrawal of offers are kept for 5 years, records on payment and supply of goods for 5 years, records on consumer complaints and dispute handling for 3 years, and records on labeling and advertising for 6 months. This applies to the extent such records exist, and not all information falls under these records.
  • Inquiry information: Destroyed without delay once the purpose of handling the inquiry is fulfilled. Any part that falls under consumer complaint and dispute records or other statutory retention is kept according to the relevant basis and period.
  • Cloud, advertising and external services: Information processed by Google is subject to the retention and deletion criteria of the relevant service. We disclose the retention periods and end criteria for Monstel's processing, provision and outsourcing, and a link to Google's policies alone does not replace the Operator's disclosure of retention periods.
  • Other logs and backups: Information whose purpose of use has ended is not kept indefinitely, and data retained by law is kept separate from general usage data and processed only for that retention purpose.

7. Destruction Procedure and Method, and Cloud Deletion

  • Information held by the Operator is destroyed without delay when it is no longer needed, for example because its purpose has been fulfilled or its period has expired. Items that must be kept under other laws are kept separately after the basis and period are confirmed, and are destroyed when that period ends.
  • The chief privacy officer confirms what is to be destroyed and any retention exceptions. Electronic files are deleted in a way that makes recovery or reproduction impossible, and paper documents, if any, are disposed of by shredding or similar means.
  • Deleting data on your device does not automatically mean that data on the verification server or in Google's cloud is deleted. If device backup or cloud sync is enabled, data may be restored, so deletion must be confirmed for each storage location.
  • For game data held by Google, you can use the management features Google provides, such as deleting this game's data from your Play Games profile. Simply disconnecting may not be the same as deleting. We explain Monstel's reset and resync behavior and the specific menu paths.
  • Requests to delete server and inquiry records held by the Operator are received at fatiger92@gmail.com. If any part cannot be deleted immediately because of statutory retention, we inform you of the items concerned, the basis, the period and the outcome.

  • Please send the type of request and the information concerned to fatiger92@gmail.com. The Operator will tell you the minimum information needed to verify your identity or your authority as an agent, and will take action and notify you of the result within the statutory deadline. If there is a legal ground for limiting or refusing the request, we will tell you the reason and how to object.
  • Access is in principle made available within 10 days of the date the request is received, and if there is a justified ground for delay or limitation, we notify you as required by law. Correction, deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent and similar requests are handled according to the procedures and deadlines of the relevant laws. The response time for general inquiries does not replace statutory deadlines for action.
  • Even where data retained by law cannot be deleted, we do not restrict the exercise of your rights over other processing across the board. Related features that become unavailable because of withdrawal of consent, disconnecting cloud save or the like are explained when we handle the request.
  • The right to request transmission of personal information, and the right to refuse or request an explanation of automated decisions that significantly affect your rights or obligations, are guaranteed to the extent the statutory requirements for their application are met.

  • The Operator must fulfill the duty to ensure security under Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act.
  • To ensure security, the Operator must put in place minimized access rights to personal information and account management, protection in transit, protection of stored information appropriate to the law and the level of risk, retention and review of access records, security updates and anti-malware measures, management of destruction and outsourcing, and other necessary administrative and physical measures.

10. Children's Personal Information and Purchases by Minors

  • Where the law requires consent to process the personal information of children under 14, we obtain the consent of the legal representative before processing and verify that consent has been given. Notices that children can easily understand, and ways for guardians to exercise their rights, must be provided.
  • If children are included in the target audience, ad SDKs and settings that comply with the Google Play Families policy must be applied. The policy requirements on restricting the transmission of advertising IDs and similar data of children and users of unknown age, restricting interest-based advertising and remarketing, ad content and format, and any necessary age verification must be met.
  • Protection of children's personal information also covers processing through SDKs. Legal representatives may contact fatiger92@gmail.com about the processing of a child's information and the exercise of rights.
  • For purchases of paid items, a minor who needs the consent of a legal representative is in principle a person under 19, in accordance with the Terms of Service and the Civil Act (민법). This is distinct from the under-14 threshold for consent to personal information processing.

11. Choices About Advertising and Automatic Collection

  • You can use features provided by the operating system, such as turning off ad personalization or resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings. Exact menu names vary by device and operating system version. We provide guidance on how to do this.
  • Turning off ad personalization or deleting your advertising ID does not stop all other processing needed for non-personalized ads, diagnostics and fraud prevention. The items, purposes and means of refusal are explained in line with the actual SDK settings.
  • If you purchase the Ad Pass, ads are not displayed. This does not mean that all SDK initialization and data transmission stop.
  • You can turn off notifications at any time in the "Notifications" row of the game's Settings or in your device's app notification settings. When they are turned off, scheduled notifications are not sent, and your use of the game is not affected.
  • There is no in-game setting to turn off sending app usage statistics. The install ID is not shown on the game screen and is deleted along with the app, and a new value is created if you reinstall. Raw data remaining on the server is automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Where separate consent for advertising is required, the necessary notice and consent are completed before collection. An opt-out setting applied afterwards on the device does not replace prior consent required by law.
  • Provider references: AdMob data disclosure, Google Play Games data collection.
